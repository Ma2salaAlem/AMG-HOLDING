AMG Steel Factory

Leading & Trusted Steel Factory with Proven QoS

AMG Steel Factory is one of the leading and most reliable steel manufacturers and suppliers in Ethiopia, established in 2010 G.C. Over the years, the company has consistently demonstrated exceptional Quality of Service (QoS), reliability, and growth through time, securing a dominant market share in the supply of steel products such as galvanized ribbed sheets, color-coated profiles, corrugated sheets, nails, and other wire products.

The Company is led by a visionary General Director, with over 25 qualified senior managers who bring rich experience to effectively manage all operations and services. Today, AMG Steel Factory employs more than 1,000 staff, including contract staff, reflecting its strong contribution to job creation and industrial development.

AMG's main plant, situated on nearly 200,000 m² of land in Adama—100 km from Addis Ababa—forms the backbone of its production capacity, while the Main Office is located in Addis Ababa, Akaki/Gelan area. With its expansion projects now fully completed and operational, the company proudly runs advanced Re-bar and Wire Rod Mills, a modern Color Coating Line, high-capacity Tube Production Lines, and a Pre-engineered Steel Fabrication Workshop. These state-of-the-art facilities significantly boost AMG's production capabilities, reinforcing its position as the best, most reliable, and customer-focused steel factory in Ethiopia.
