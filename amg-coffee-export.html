AMG Coffee Export

#One Coffee Exporter in Ethiopia, Trusted Worldwide.

Founded in 2011 G.C., AMG Coffee Export is one of the leading and most well-known exporters in Ethiopia, with more than a decade of experience in supplying premium organic coffee, oilseeds, and pulses to international markets. Our export destinations include Europe, the USA, & Asia, where AMG has earned a strong reputation for delivering consistent quality and reliability.

As the number one coffee exporter in Ethiopia and a key contributor to the country's foreign currency earnings, AMG Coffee Export plays a vital role in strengthening Ethiopia's economy while showcasing the rich agricultural heritage of the nation. Built on a foundation of trust and long-term partnerships, the company has been widely recognized by global business partners for its uncompromising standards in both products and services.

The company is led by its CEO and founder, whose vision and leadership have shaped AMG into a trusted global brand. Today, AMG employs more than 200 skilled staff and operates its own state-of-the-art coffee cleaning facilities in Addis Ababa, as well as a pulse and cereal cleaning facility in Adama, 100 km from the capital. The company's headquarters is also located in Addis Ababa.

Through dedication, quality, and reliability, AMG Coffee Export continues to stand as Ethiopia's best and most recognized partner in the global export market.
